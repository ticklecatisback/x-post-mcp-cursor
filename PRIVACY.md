# Privacy notice — private beta

The hosted service processes the minimum information needed to connect an X account and publish an
explicitly confirmed post: X account ID, display name, username, encrypted OAuth credentials, the
prepared post text, confirmation metadata, and the returned post ID.

Prepared drafts expire automatically. OAuth credentials are encrypted at rest. The service does
not sell personal information or use connected-account content to train models.

Users can invoke `x_disconnect` to remove the stored X connection and revoke the active MCP
session. Production publication requires adding the operator's legal identity, privacy contact,
retention period, hosting region, subprocessors, and deletion-request procedure.
